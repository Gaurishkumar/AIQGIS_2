about=The plugin imports the client's survey workbook, sends the records to a local AI/GIS worker (127.0.0.1) that matches each survey point to a building (historic polygons and Overture footprints, scored by distance, name, address, road side, plausibility and source), refines it with SAM 2.1 when licence-approved imagery exists, validates and routes it. Proposals are real features in an editable GeoPackage; operators approve, edit, click-to-fix, pick another candidate, draw manually or reject, and export a copy of the workbook with corrected coordinates. The plugin itself never imports PyTorch.
